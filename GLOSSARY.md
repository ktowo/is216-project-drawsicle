# Drawsicle

Online art exchanges (Secret Santas and art trades) for hobby communities: organisers run events, artists offer their characters and make gifts for each other.

## Language

**Character**:
An original character in someone's private library, offered to artists in exchanges.
_Avoid_: OC (in the UI), avatar

**Colours**:
The up-to-six swatches that define how a character looks; the character's screen pet takes its colours from them too.
_Avoid_: Palette (reserved for a pet's preset colour scheme), swatch set

**Share card**:
An image of one event (its stage, dates, real counts and a QR code to its page) that anyone allowed to share the event can download or post.
_Avoid_: Hype card, poster, banner (the event's own header image)

**Holo view**:
A full-screen, tilting holographic card of one gift's picture in the museum, with a sparkle shimmer that follows the light.
_Avoid_: Foil card, NFT view, trading card

**Identity**:
Who someone proved to be when signing in (with Google, Discord or an email and password); one account can have several linked identities.
_Avoid_: Login, provider account

**Account**:
A person's place in Drawsicle: their profile, characters, exchanges and settings, reached through any of their linked identities.
_Avoid_: User (in the UI), profile (that's the public page)

**Email type**:
A kind of email someone can get (deadline reminders, matches, event updates and so on), each set to instant, daily digest or off.
_Avoid_: Notification category, channel

**Daily digest**:
One email a day gathering everything of the email types set to digest.
_Avoid_: Summary email, newsletter
