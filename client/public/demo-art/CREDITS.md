# Demo artwork

These pictures stand in for the Spring Bloom Exchange gifts in the demo data (`shared/demo-data.mjs`).
They are **AI-generated placeholders** (Google Gemini, October 2026), made from prompts describing the
demo characters. They are not the work of any real artist, and the demo artists and their exchanges are
fictional.

Many art communities don't allow AI-generated art, so these are only for demonstrating the app. Replace
them with human-made art (for example, classmates' drawings shared with permission and credited here) by
keeping the same file names.
